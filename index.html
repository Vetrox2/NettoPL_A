    How it's calculated

    • Social security (ZUS): pension 9.76%, disability 1.5%, sickness 2.45% of gross. Pension and disability stop once your gross for the year passes PLN (30× the projected average wage).
    • Health insurance: 9% of gross minus social security. It is not deductible from tax.
    • Tax base: gross minus social security minus work costs (250 PLN, or 300 PLN when commuting), rounded to full złoty.
    • Author's costs: for the creative share of your salary, 50% of that share minus the social security paid on it, up to PLN per year. The standard work costs cover the rest of the salary; if all of it is creative, only author's costs apply.
    • PIT advance: 12% of the tax base, 32% on the part above PLN for the year, minus 300 PLN with PIT-2, rounded to full złoty.
    • Relief for young people: pay up to PLN per year is exempt from PIT. Social security and health insurance are still paid. Above the limit, only the excess is taxed.

    Salaries in another currency are converted to złoty at the NBP average rate (table A). Tax and contributions are always calculated in złoty, so limits like 120 000 PLN stay in złoty. For the real payslip, your employer uses the NBP rate from the last business day before payday, so the result will move with the exchange rate.

    This is an estimate of the monthly payslip for one employer and a constant salary. It doesn't cover PPK, bonuses, sick leave, joint filing with a spouse or other reliefs settled in your annual PIT return. Your employer's numbers may differ by a few grosze due to rounding.
